## What the form is (and is not)

`CAR FORMAT` is BoG's **pre-CRD adjusted-capital-base / adjusted-asset-base** computation, not a Basel II/III RWA return:

- **ADJUSTED CAPITAL BASE (E25)** = Tier 1 (paid-up capital + disclosed reserves + permanent preference shares) − Tier 1 deductions (goodwill/intangibles, losses not provided for, investments in unconsolidated subsidiaries, investments in the capital of other banks/FIs, connected lending of a long-term nature) + Tier 2 (undisclosed reserves, revaluation reserves, subordinated term debt, hybrid capital). Rows 12–13 (Capitalised / Latent Revaluation Reserves) have item numbers but **no amount cell** in the official workbook and are outside E24's `SUM(E20:E23)`; the Guide's Tier 2 "cumulative preference shares" have no row of their own on the solo form (they are hybrid instruments here); **general provisions have no line at all** (the pre-CRD form predates their Tier 2 admission), nor do CET1 deductions such as deferred tax assets.
- **ADJUSTED ASSET BASE (E69)** = TOTAL ASSETS − cash − claims on BoG − claims on Government − the printed percentage of the partially weighted classes (80% of cheques in clearing, 80% of claims on discount houses / other banks, 50% of claims on public-sector FIs, 80% of government- / multilateral-guaranteed loans, 50% of residential mortgages, 50% of export finance) − the four capital-deduction items again on the asset side + net contingent liabilities (Σ contingents − 50% of class-1 − 80% of class-2 items) + **50% of NOP** + **100% of the 3-year average annual gross income**. Every percentage is printed in the row label and restated in `NEW RISK WEIGHTS` — the map applies exactly those, never a platform weight.
- **E70 `=E25/E69`** is the ratio (a fraction; the sheet's title says "as percentage … X 100" — the template's own formula does not multiply) and **E71 `=E25-(E69*6%)`** the surplus/deficit test at the 6% printed in the formula (the label says 10%: the template's formula wins, reproduced verbatim). Every roll-up (E10, E17, E24, E25, E31, E38, E53, E54, E65, E69) is the template's own formula, evaluated over the inputs below — never re-implemented.

**Why the BSD5A ratio is not the platform's Basel III CAR (by construction, not by accident).** The capital engine (`regulatory_capital` / `app/domain/capital`) computes CAR = total capital / (credit RWA + market RWA + operational RWA) with the standardised weights, the FX charge × 12.5 and the BIA charge × 12.5. BSD5A adds 50% of the same NOP and 100% of the same 3-year average gross income, weights credit by BoG's printed classes, admits no general provisions and deducts no DTA. On the hermetic book: BSD5A E25 = 340.0 (= the run's total capital, because the fixture's DTA deduction and general provisions happen to offset), E69 = 2,080.83 vs run RWA 2,147.5 → E70 = 16.34% vs run CAR 15.83%. The test proves the add-ons reconcile exactly (market RWA = 2 × E67 × fx% × multiplier; operational RWA = E68 × α × multiplier) and asserts the two ratios differ. Do not "fix" one to equal the other: both are correct under their own rules.

## Platform sources — what each block is read from

- **Capital constituents** — `capital_component` facts (fact_derivation `_derive_capital_components`: one fact per lower-cased `capital_component` name of the bank's `capital_structure` register, `capital_tier` CET1/AT1/T2, `is_deduction` from a `_DEDUCTION` tier suffix or a negative amount). Resolver `bsd5.capital_facts` selects by the **name vocabulary** in `linemaps/bsd5a.py` (`PAID_UP`, `PERMANENT_PREFERENCE`, `UNDISCLOSED`, `REVALUATION`, `GOODWILL`, `LOSSES_NOT_PROVIDED`, `INVESTMENTS_SUBSIDIARIES`, `INVESTMENTS_OTHER_BANKS`, `CONNECTED_LENDING`, `SUBORDINATED_DEBT`, `HYBRID`, `CUMULATIVE_PREFERENCE`, plus `CAPITALISED_REVALUATION`, `LATENT_REVALUATION`, `MINORITY*` used by BSD5B) and/or by **tier** (row 2 = CET1 minus every named category; row 3 = AT1 minus hybrids/cumulative prefs). Positive rows take non-deduction facts (deduction-flagged facts of the `RESERVE_DEFICITS` names — income surplus / retained earnings deficits — enter row 2 negatively); deduction rows take deduction-flagged facts as positive amounts, which the template subtracts. A component whose name is unknown AND whose tier is T2 (e.g. `general_provisions`) or which is a CET1 deduction with no row (e.g. `deferred_tax_assets`) has **no BSD5A line** and is reported nowhere on this form (see "Residual").
- **TOTAL ASSETS** — `bsd5.balance_sheet_side` = Σ `balance_sheet` facts with `attributes.side == asset` (cash_vault, bog_required_reserves, bog_excess_reserves, securities_bog_bills, securities_gog_bonds, loans_gross, other_assets) — the same on-balance-sheet total the capital engine's leverage exposure uses. Contra items are not carried in the platform balance sheet. Zero-weight rows are the individual balance-sheet facts; note the platform's securities split is by instrument (bills vs bonds), so its `securities_bog_bills` / `securities_gog_bonds` category names are the issuer attribution BSD5A reports (rows 23.iv / 24.i) — the same attribution the capital engine's zero-weight lines use.
- **Partially weighted classes** — `bsd5.pct_of` applies the row's printed percentage to `positions.sum` (claims on discount houses / other banks by canonical counterparty type and `institution_class`; cheques in clearing by `instrument`) or to the `loan_exposure` `residential_mortgage` fact.
- **Contingent liabilities** — the platform's LC/guarantee book is `off_balance` facts (Σ notional of LC_GUARANTEE positions, `committed_retail` / `committed_corporate`); positions tagged with the documented `obs_category=letter_of_credit` attribute (docs/API_INTEGRATION.md §3.4) report on row 38, the remainder on row 39 (`bsd5.off_balance_residual` — the platform's liquidity return classifies untagged LC/guarantee positions as indemnities and guarantees, the same default). Acceptances, endorsements, RUF/NIF, standby LCs and the class-1/class-2 split have no canonical tag → input_required.
- **50% of NOP** — `bsd5.pct_of` 50 × `bsd5.run_line`(`market_rwa`/`fx_charge`/`exposure_amount`) = the larger net open FX position the latest SUCCEEDED capital baseline run struck its market-risk charge on; **3-year average gross income** — `bsd5.avg_gross_income` = arithmetic mean of the run's `gross_income_<year>` operational-risk lines (latest 3 years). Both are input_required until the capital engine has run for the period ("run the capital baseline first").
- **NEW RISK WEIGHTS / PROVISION / item numbers** — `constant` = the template's own printed value, read from the committed layout at import (`template_constants`) so a BoG weight can never be retyped; `unscaled`. The platform's `ParamRiskWeight` set is keyed by generic codes (RW0/RW20/RW35/RW50/RW75/RW100/RW150), not by BoG line item, and the platform holds no BoG provisioning-rate ladder (IFRS 9 `ParamEclAssumption` rows are stage PD/LGD), so no run-derived value is substituted anywhere on those two sheets.
