Band arithmetic (Guide, BSD6 "Maturity"): calendar months from the reporting date; a month-end reporting date maps to month-ends (31 Mar + 1 → 30 Apr, 30 Jun + 1 → 31 Jul), the boundary date belongs to the LATER band, exactly the Guide's worked table (31 Mar: "<1 month" = 1–29 Apr, "1–<3 months" = 30 Apr–29 Jun, "3–<6" = 30 Jun–29 Sep, "6–<12" = 30 Sep–30 Mar; likewise for 30 Jun / 30 Sep / 31 Dec). A maturity on a Saturday/Sunday counts as the following Monday ("succeeding business day"; public holidays are not modelled). ## Placement rules applied (all from the Guide's BSD6 notes)

- **Dated positions** (`contractual_maturity`) → the band containing the (weekend-rolled) maturity; assets due ≥ 14 days before the reporting date → Overdue, < 14 days → less than 1 month; liabilities already due → less than 1 month (earliest repayment date).
- **Deposits** → earliest repayment date: a demand/savings deposit without a contractual date uses the enriched `behavioral_maturity_months` when present (Guide 15 & 16: historical withdrawal pattern, agreed with BSD), otherwise less than 1 month (on demand).
- **Provisions for bad debts and interest in suspense** → Overdue (BSD6A/B row 29 total debt provision; row 30 stays input_required with the note).
- **Cash on hand, sight balances** → Overdue; **Reserves** → 5 years and over, negative → Overdue.
- **Undated positions** (platform ladder convention, `regulatory_liquidity._ladder_bucket_index`): cash → Overdue (Guide), money at call → less than 1 month, everything else → 5 years and over.
- **Fact-sourced BSD2 lines with no Guide band** (Other Assets) → FROM BSD2 and Total fill, the eight band cells stay blank (`input_required`) for the bank to allocate.
- **Sub-total rows** (loans Sub-total, section totals, Total Assets, Shareholders' Funds, the liabilities total, Assets − Liabilities) are the template's own formulas per column.

### Interpretations recorded (not resolved by guesswork)

- The Guide's note "Reserves – 5 years and above; if negative → overdue" names no BSD2 line. It is applied to BOTH readings — statutory reserve balances with BoG (BSD2 17 *Special deposits*, BSD6 row 8) and shareholders' Reserves / Paid-up capital (BSD6 rows 41–47) — because each is a perpetual / undated non-demand item the platform's liquidity ladder already places in the longest band; both placements are stated on the row's note.
- Paid-up capital has no explicit Guide band; it is placed with Reserves (Shareholders' Funds row 48 = 41 + 42).
- Partial arrears (an overdue instalment on a loan that has not matured) need arrears/instalment data the canonical snapshot does not carry (`attributes` only); today the Overdue column holds matured-but-outstanding principal, provisions and interest in suspense.
- Business-day roll uses weekends only (no Ghana holiday calendar).

## Cross-form dependencies

- `BSD6A!B*` = `BSD2!B*` (Domestic column) and `BSD6B!B*` = `BSD2!C*` (Foreign column) of the same reporting date — resolved from the computed BSD2 (`depends_on=("BSD2",)`; the generator computes BSD2 first). Every platform-fed Total equals its FROM BSD2 cell by construction (proven for all 84 fed rows in `tests/services/bog_forms/test_bsd6.py`).
- Because the map imports BSD2's line map, a BSD2 correction (filters, signs, new sources) flows into BSD6 automatically; a BSD2 line that becomes `input_required` makes the BSD6 row(s) it feeds `input_required` too.
- BSD2 lines with no BSD6 row (template design, recorded not fixed): BSD2 A.3–A.5 (other claims on / loans to / equity in non-residents) and, on BSD6B, the Foreign column of the DOMESTIC borrowing/deposit sections (BSD2 21–27) — BSD6B's borrowing/deposit rows read only the FOREIGN LIABILITIES section (BSD2 18–20).

## Framework asks (things outside this form's files)

- `sources.positions.sum` compares JSON attributes as TEXT (`attributes[key].as_string() == str(value)`): integer/boolean `attribute_eq` values in `linemaps/bsd2.py` (`tenor_days: 91`, `tenor_years: 2`, `long_term: True`) never match on SQLite and booleans never match on Postgres either (`'True'` vs `true`). BSD6 inherits the filters unchanged; the BSD6 test stores the text form. Fix belongs in `positions.sum` (or normalise the BSD2 params to strings).
- `positions.sum`'s Foreign column sums raw foreign-currency `balance` (no `balance_ghs` conversion). BSD6B mirrors it so its Total = BSD2 Foreign holds; when `positions.sum` adopts the ingested cedi leg, `sources_ext/bsd6.py::_position_rows` should switch with it (single place: the `measure` column).
- BSD2 map issues BSD6 inherits by design (owner: BSD2): row 69 provisions use `sign=-1` although the template already subtracts (`B60 = B68 − SUM(B69:B71)`); capital rows 128–134 use `currency="all"` so BSD2's Domestic and Foreign columns both carry the full amount (BSD6B rows 41–47 therefore show cedi capital); rows 180–183 (22. Cheques for clearing) are bound to call-money borrowings; deposits 148–166 (non-resident section) carry no `resident=False` filter while 228–258 require `attribute block=26`.
- No "Annex 1" sheet exists in the BSD6 workbook for unmatured spot/forward FX; if BoG supplies one, forwards would come from `FX_HEDGE`/`DERIVATIVE` positions (`leg` attribute) — nothing to bind today.
